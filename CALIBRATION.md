# Calibration

Global Matrix-WP calibration starts from the supplied baseline buckets and is updated only by eligible normal completions and in-match retirements. Pre-match withdrawal, walkover, and cancellation create visible `YELLOW_NON_GRADED` entries, advance the master sequence, and do not change bucket numerators or denominators.

Matrix calibration and audit-color learning are separate ledgers. Historical Verified Win Rate is never presented as the current independent match probability. Small samples, including 2/2, are explicitly labelled.
