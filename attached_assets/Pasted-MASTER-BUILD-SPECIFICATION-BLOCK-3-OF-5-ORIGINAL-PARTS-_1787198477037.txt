PART 23 — RETIREMENT / WALKOVER GRADING
============================================================

IN_MATCH_RETIREMENT:

If match started and player later retires:

GRADE AS NORMAL COMPLETED MATCH.

Official winning/advancing player = winner.

Prediction result:

WIN or LOSS.

Count in calibration numerator/denominator and Verified Win Rate.

Preserve:

RESULT_TYPE=IN_MATCH_RETIREMENT.

PRE-MATCH retirement/withdrawal:

NO WIN/LOSS grading.

WALKOVER:

NO WIN/LOSS grading.

CANCELLATION:

NO WIN/LOSS grading.

Instead create:

YELLOW_NON_GRADED

Reason codes:

YELLOW_PREMATCH_WITHDRAWAL
YELLOW_WALKOVER
YELLOW_CANCELLATION

Keep visible in master history.

Do NOT enter calibration wins/losses/numerator/denominator.
