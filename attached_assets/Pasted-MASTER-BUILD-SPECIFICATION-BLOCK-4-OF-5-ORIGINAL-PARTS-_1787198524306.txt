============================================================
PART 31 — GREEN / DOUBLE GREEN
============================================================

GREEN requires:

complete independent P1/P2 audit
minimum effective independent evidence families
Matrix-removal survival
reasonable strongest-family-removal survival
complete Dangerous Underdog Audit
no multiple high-quality opponent pathways
no unresolved critical contradiction
sufficient critical family coverage
fresh critical data

Matrix-derived evidence cannot satisfy independent minimum.

DOUBLE GREEN requires every Green condition plus stricter active-audit requirements:

larger independent-family count
stronger stress-test survival
stronger removal stability
minimal dangerous-underdog ceiling
high-quality critical coverage

Calibration alone cannot create Double Green.

Market alone cannot create Double Green.

Matrix alone cannot create Double Green.

PART 32 — YELLOW / RED
============================================================

Audit YELLOW may result from:

meaningful conflict
thin sample
weak separation
current/historical conflict
meaningful opponent pathway
removal instability
incomplete noncritical evidence
other active audit rules

RED/PASS may result from:

multiple independent contradictions
near coin flip
dangerous opponent survives
critical evidence problems
Matrix favorite failing independent removal
severe instability
active audit vetoes

RED does not require winner flip.

PASS is valid.

Never force a selection merely because tennis has two players.
